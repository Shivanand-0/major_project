Formaldehyde (HCHO) is a direct intermediate product of volatile organic compound (VOC) photolysis emitted from high-temperature biomass combustion. The strong correlation (r = 0.735, ρ = 0.962) validates that satellite-observed formaldehyde spikes over India are directly driven by active fire radiative intensity.

Dominant Transport Axis NW ➔ SE Punjab ➔ UP ➔ Bay of Bengal

Post-monsoon synoptic winds predominantly blow from northwest to southeast along the Indo-Gangetic trough. In calm, nocturnal inversion conditions, plumes accumulate near source farmlands and forest fringes, whereas persistent daytime northwesterly winds transport elevated HCHO concentrations downwind across Eastern India.
